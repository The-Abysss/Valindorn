The Game

Valindorn is a fantasy adventure game where exploration, magic, combat, character growth, and player choices all come together.

⚔️ Combat

Fight enemies, defend yourself, use spells, manage your resources, and decide when to escape.

🗺️ Exploration

Travel between towns and explore the world beyond combat.

👥 NPCs

Talk with characters who have their own roles, personalities, and stories.

📜 Contracts

Gods and devils can offer powerful contracts, but a character cannot hold both paths at the same time.

Stats & Leveling

Characters grow through levels. When a character gains a level, they receive stat points that can be spent however the player chooses.

💪 Strength

Improves physical power and the effectiveness of physical attacks.

🛡️ Endurance

Improves durability and helps a character withstand damage.

✨ Spirit

Improves the effectiveness of elemental magic. Spirit affects elemental magic only.

⚡ Agility

Improves speed, reflexes, and other abilities related to quick movement.

Player-Controlled Growth

Leveling does not automatically decide how your character develops. The player chooses where their available stat points go.

This allows different characters to develop in very different ways.

Magic System

Magic in Valindorn is divided into elemental magic and faith-based magic. Different spells can have different purposes, including combat, healing, support, purification, curses, and other effects.

🔥 Fire

Fire, Lightning, Solar

💧 Water

Water, Ice, Blood

🌿 Earth

Plant, Rock, Crystal

🌪️ Air

Wind, Pressure, Sound

✨ Holy

Healing, Purification, Protection, and other supportive effects.

🌑 Shadow

Curses, Debuffs, Shadow effects, and other abilities.

Magic Beyond Combat

Not every spell is a combat spell. Healing and other useful magic can be used while exploring or outside of combat when the spell allows it.

A spell's effect determines how it can be used rather than automatically making every spell combat-only.

Birthday Affinity

Every character's birthday determines their natural elemental affinity.

Each exact calendar date has a fixed affinity profile. The same birthday will always produce the same elemental percentages.

Fire Water Earth Air No Affinity

The four elemental percentages do not have to add up to 100%. Any remaining percentage is No Affinity.

Birthday affinity determines a character's natural elemental connection. Spirit is a separate stat that affects the power of elemental magic.

Personality AI

Valindorn learns about the player's behavior through meaningful choices made during the game.

⚖️ Starting Point

Every character begins at exactly 50% Holy and 50% Shadow.

🧠 Learning

The game records meaningful choices and looks for patterns in the player's behavior.

✨ Holy

Choices that align with the game's Holy personality factors can influence the character's Holy percentage.

🌑 Shadow

Shadow percentage is calculated from the Holy percentage, keeping Holy and Shadow at exactly 100% combined.

Holy & Shadow Are Separate From Elemental Affinity

A character's elemental affinity does not determine whether they are Holy or Shadow. The Personality AI learns that aspect from the player's behavior.

Spirit affects elemental magic, while the Holy/Shadow balance represents a different part of the character's development.
