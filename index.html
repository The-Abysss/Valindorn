Welcome to Valindorn

Explore a fantasy world where your choices help shape the kind of magic your character can wield.

The Game

Valindorn is a fantasy adventure game focused on exploration, magic, combat, towns, characters, and player choices.

Magic System

Elemental Magic

Fire, Water, Earth, and Air each contain multiple forms of magic.

Holy Magic

Healing, Purification, and Buffing.

Shadow Magic

Shadow, Cursing, and Debuffing.

Personality AI

Valindorn's personality system learns from the player's actions instead of asking the player to choose a predetermined alignment.

The player begins with a balanced 50% Holy / 50% Shadow. As the player makes choices throughout the game, the personality system analyzes those choices and adjusts the Holy percentage.

Shadow percentage is always calculated as the remainder, keeping the two values at exactly 100%.
